<h1>About me</h1>

<p>
  I'm a research methodologist and applied economist based in Chicago. My work sits
  at the intersection of quantitative research and causal inference &mdash; survey
  design, program evaluation, and applying rigorous methods to real-world questions.
</p>

<h2>Background</h2>
<p>

  fieldwork spans more than 20 countries across Sub-Saharan Africa.
</p>
